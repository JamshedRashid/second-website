# Storefront entrance — reference and implementation notes

Reference: supplied 24.94-second MP4. The website occupies a smaller region within a portrait social clip, so exact original measurements and hover behavior cannot be recovered. The implementation follows the visible composition and motion language, adapted to a mobile store.

## Observed sequence

- 0–3 seconds: cream canvas, compact navigation, centered headline, scattered floating objects; a phone rises into view.
- 3–5 seconds: bright lime section, central phone and supporting text.
- 5–9 seconds: charcoal canvas with light rounded cards; staggered card/content reveals and a taller asymmetric card grid.
- 9–12 seconds: lime field; circular objects spread outward around centered copy.
- 12–14 seconds: dark central phone composition with floating annotation chips.
- 14–19 seconds: cream split panels, alternating image/text sides; large rounded media panels slide between stories.
- 19–21 seconds: dark footer, grouped rounded panels, a wide looping lime ribbon.

## Store adaptation

- A light glass navigation bar and centered entrance with floating category objects, a rising phone, restrained orbit lines, motion pause, and scroll cue.
- A charcoal category grid for Phones, Sound, Charging, Cases, and the full collection.
- A lime connected-essentials section with floating products.
- Featured phones use the existing catalogue records, images, and illustrative prices.
- A dark Colour Studio teaser leads to selecting a phone. Individual phone links open the existing configuration studio.
- Three desktop sticky stories switch as the user scrolls; mobile and reduced-motion layouts show each story sequentially.
- Shopping guidance, expandable questions, and a glass footer with looping ribbon finish the homepage.
- Hover effects are designed additions: lift, product rotation, soft highlight sweep, and arrow movement. They are not claimed to be exact reconstructions of unshown reference interactions.

## Preserved behavior

Catalogue records and assets, phone colour/storage options, background mood controls, full view, filters, configured cart items, bag, and demo checkout are retained. Home now renders the storefront. Phone detail routes render the studio. The studio model selector updates its route without discarding the surrounding implementation.

No new inventory/specification claims, testimonials, shipping promises, contact details, or real purchasing services were added. Existing illustrative product data remains unchanged.
